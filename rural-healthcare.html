Common Diseases & Symptoms

⚠ This is general information only, not a diagnosis. Please consult a doctor.

Go to hospital immediately if…

  • Chest pain, breathlessness, or fainting
  • Heavy bleeding, severe burns, or accident injury
  • Snake / dog bite, poisoning
  • Stroke signs: face drooping, arm weakness, slurred speech
  • Pregnancy: labour pain, bleeding, or no baby movement
  • High fever with seizures, or an unconscious person

While waiting for the ambulance: keep the patient calm, lying down, loosen tight clothes, and do not give food or water if unconscious.
